          How the tap actually works
          
Phones cannot reliably push a custom encrypted file to each other over raw NFC anymore. Android Beam is gone, and iPhone apps are not allowed to do general phone-to-phone NFC data dump.

          
What works today: write the encrypted blob to a cheap NFC sticker (in a case, keyring, or wallet). Mate taps the sticker. Or flash the QR. Or hand them the tiny .tapenc file via AirDrop / Quick Share after the tap handshake those systems already provide.

          
Web NFC write/read works in Chrome on Android only, and only against tags — not against another phone acting as a tag.
